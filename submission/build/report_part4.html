10.4 Stored evidence, kept apart by kind

Below the boundary statement ("Degradation appears to begin at or after Hop 3. This is the earliest hop whose onset is corroborated in the stored data. It is a statement about when changes were observed, not a finding about the reason for them.") the page separates the evidence into measured facts, inference, temporal correlation and unknown, and shows the stored confidence with its limits: level and score, an explicit statement that it is a fixed heuristic and not a probability, the corroborating and unknown hops, and the completeness fraction.

10.5 Guided explanation mode

The Guided explanation toggle (off by default) adds a plain-language walkthrough built only from the selected bundle: it states the symptom the application saw, what each hop shows, what the stored result infers, how far to trust it, and what the result does not show. A five-step walkthrough (Previous / Next / Exit) moves through the same material and can scroll to the matching part of the page. A clean-control trial is explained in distinct wording (no degradation detected, which does not prove health).

Further captures (stored evaluation, limitations, the no-degradation result and the partially-localized result) are in Appendix D.

12.4 Analysis

  • What changed between Phase 4 and 4b (on the exposed set): the two clean-control false positives disappeared and one jitter trial (08) became localized. Nothing changed for loss or bandwidth, and p4-held-12 remained a false attribution. The improvement is consistent with the diagnosed mechanism (single noisy buckets crossing a near-zero absolute threshold), but it cannot be separated from the fact that the design saw these traces.
  • Latency and jitter were localized when a claim was made (latency 1 of 1; jitter 3 of 3 under Phase 4b, one of them a false attribution). Loss and bandwidth were localized in 0 of 4 held-out trials (by both detectors) and 0 of 4 Phase 4b tuning trials (by all four candidates). A possible reason, that bucketed RTT may be the wrong observable for these faults, is recorded in the project as an open problem, not as a finding.
  • The false attribution in p4-held-12 occurred where the first affected hop (Hop 2) was itself unknown; the detector reported the next hop. The system correctly lowered confidence to medium and shows Hops 1 and 2 as unknown, but under the project's own definition (a confident claim that is wrong) it is a false attribution.
  • Small samples. With 12 trials, one trial moves a rate by 8.3 percentage points; "within ±1 hop 4/4" has a 95% interval of 51% to 100%; four clean controls cannot distinguish 0% from nearly 50% false positives. The specification's 120-trial tuning set and 288-cell evaluation matrix were not run.

12.5 The recorded demo run (not an evaluation)

demo-run-01 is a recorded real run: a 90 ms latency impairment on the link leaving Hop 2, injected 25 s into a 35 s capture. The frozen mad_sustained pipeline reported "Degradation appears to begin at or after Hop 3", high confidence (score 1.0), corroborating Hops 3, 4 and 5, with the application and Hop 3 onsets both at +25.0 s (Figure 4, Figure 7). From the recorded files: Hop 3 probes averaged 78.53 ms over the last third of the faulted capture and 0.012 ms in a fresh 10-round probe after the fault was cleared (recovery_hop.jsonl). The scenario was chosen in the detector's favourable regime (fault covering a minority of the window) and illustrates the pipeline; it is not evidence about detector accuracy.
